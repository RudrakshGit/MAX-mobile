## Owner decisions that still apply

| When | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Oct 2026 | Optional Google login may keep app data on the server, so progress survives a new phone. All ages allowed |
| Oct 2026 | Only verified, free-to-use self-checks; ISI and PSS-10 are licensed and stay out |
| Oct 2026 | Visual world: black ink on warm paper, hand-drawn, the Help red as the only alarm colour |
| 7 Nov 2026 | No springs or bounce. Colours: Help red, MAX's pink cheeks and soft card tints only, never for text |
| 7 Nov 2026 | Tabs are Today · Explore · Talk · Journal; MAX is drawn in code as a rig, not AI-generated art |
| 11 Nov 2026 | Buttons, chips and the Help pill are plain rounded boxes; only card outlines, the text-field line and MAX look hand-drawn |
| 11 Nov 2026 | College dashboard lives on the website; the app keeps only "Share stats with college" and an optional College ID |
| 11 Nov 2026 | The web build is a clearly marked demo with limits (20 chats a day, no native features) |
| 11 Nov 2026 | Cards animate only while in view, one at a time; avatars, like MAX, keep their light colours in dark mode |
| 11 Nov 2026 | MAX is MAX (Mental-health Assistance and eXpert), not "the app's AI"; it never assumes how the student feels |
| 11 Nov 2026 | After a helpline card MAX keeps talking in care mode for at most two replies, then talks normally |
